# P01 — Scientific contract

**Status: NOT_APPLICABLE.**

P01 stores registry facts entered by the owner. It computes no engineering value:
- Life counters are only stored as entered (source MANUAL).
- Engine model, variant and thrust rating are recorded only with owner-supplied evidence. Nothing is inferred.
- No OEM constant enters the registry.

Gates:
- **Regression gate:** not applicable.
- **Correctness gate:** not applicable.
- **Approval gate:** not applicable.

**Open data input:** I-01 is the real fleet (registrations, ESNs, installation history). Until it arrives, demonstrations use test-labelled ESNs.
