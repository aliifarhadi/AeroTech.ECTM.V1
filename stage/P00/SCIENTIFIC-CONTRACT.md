# P00 — Scientific contract

**Status: NOT_APPLICABLE.**

P00 computes no engineering number. It has no inputs, units, methods, assumptions or independent vectors.

The only arithmetic in P00 is the SHA-256 of configuration document content and of fixture files. That is an integrity check, not a scientific result.

Gates:
- **Regression gate:** not applicable.
- **Correctness gate:** not applicable.
- **Approval gate:** not applicable.

`SOFTWARE_VERIFIED` is reported separately from `ENGINEERING_APPROVED`. `ENGINEERING_APPROVED` is not applicable to P00.
